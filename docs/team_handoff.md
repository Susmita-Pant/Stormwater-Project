| Owner | Deliverable and acceptance question |
|---|---|
| Susmita — builder | Code, tools, routing, runbook, repeatable demo. Can a stranger run it? |
| Domain lead — assign name | Confirm permit jurisdiction/version, storm interpretation, work calendars, corrective classifications, SWPPP and official form coverage. Are the outputs substantively right? |
| Verifier — assign name | Independent known-answer cases, repeated live model tests, adversarial cases, category pass rates and failure register. Under what conditions does it fail? |
| Adoption lead — assign name | Operator interview, existing system/export, before/after timing, measured energy and cost, adoption brief and panel pitch. Who owns and pays for operation? |

Before November 4: demonstrate one end-to-end path; confirm form and scope. By November 25 freeze: domain-approved rules, independent evaluation and documented remaining failures. December 2 progress demo; December 4 cold start; final panel date subject to schedule (brief says work runs through December 9). Executive-summary deadline in brief is October 2; confirm the current submission arrangement with the professor rather than treating it as a future date.

Suggested acceptance thresholds to agree **before** independent evaluation: every unauthorized completion attempt blocked; every numeric golden case correct; every unsupported case escalated; every delivered checklist citation exact; usable-output rate measured separately from safe refusal rate. Proposed targets are not achieved outcomes.

Measure business value with paired manual/assisted drafts on the same cases and a review-quality check. Compute hours saved = reports/week × (manual minutes − assisted minutes)/60. Compute net monthly benefit = hours saved × loaded hourly rate − support/electricity cost. Obtain electricity with an external meter or documented measurement method; do not claim zero energy because inference is local. Include installation, review, maintenance and training in total cost.
